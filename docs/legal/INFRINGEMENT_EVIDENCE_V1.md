# VOID Infringement Evidence V1

`VOID_INFRINGEMENT_EVIDENCE_V1`

SPDX-License-Identifier: VCL-1.0

## Purpose

Create a reproducible, human-reviewed evidence packet for suspected copying,
license breach, or VOID brand impersonation.

The evidence collector is read-only. It must never exploit, disrupt, disable,
probe private systems, obtain credentials, or submit an automatic complaint.

## Required packet fields

1. Case identifier and UTC capture time.
2. Reporter identity and review status.
3. Public repository, package, domain, application, or network location.
4. Exact source URLs and immutable commit, release, archive, or content hashes.
5. File-level and line-level similarity evidence.
6. Matching non-secret VOID forensic markers.
7. Copyright and license notices present, changed, or removed.
8. VOID names, logos, chain identifiers, genesis claims, and affiliation claims.
9. Public screenshots or archived public pages.
10. The exact VCL version and provision believed to apply.
11. Classification as authorized, insufficient, license concern, copyright
    concern, trademark concern, impersonation concern, or independent implementation.
12. Human legal-review approval or rejection.
13. Any notice sent and any response or counter-notice.

## Enforcement boundary

No DMCA notice, trademark complaint, registrar complaint, hosting report, cease
and desist, or litigation referral may be generated or submitted automatically.

A qualified human must verify ownership, protected material, copied expression,
license scope, jurisdiction, and the accuracy of every sworn statement.
