## Living Constitution Doctrine

The VOID Constitution is a **living constitutional instrument**. It is expected to evolve as VOID matures, gains evidence, encounters new conditions, and develops more capable institutions.

Living does not mean informal or arbitrary. A conversation, custom, AI inference, repository edit, validator preference, technical majority, implementation detail, or repeated practice does not become constitutional law merely because it exists.

While constitutional authority remains Sovereign-held, a constitutional change becomes authoritative only through a valid Sovereign amendment, replacement, ratification, or repeal under the authentication and authority rules of the Crown layer. After a valid Sovereign relinquishment, the validator-held amendment rules and entrenched duties of the parent instrument control.

Each constitutional change should be written, attributable, versioned, and preserved with enough provenance to distinguish historical text from the current valid rule. Machine-readable fixtures and focused proofs should be used where practical so important constitutional facts are not left to implication.

Until a valid amendment changes a rule, the current valid constitutional text remains in force. Evolution is therefore deliberate continuity, not silent drift.

## Primary Long-Range Mission — Peaceful Propagation

VOID's **primary long-range mission** is to preserve and peacefully seed or propagate VOID and its Constitution beyond Earth and, as civilization and physical possibility permit, across the universe.

That mission includes preserving constitutional knowledge, continuity, participating life, auditable history, and the ability of future willing communities and agents to establish compatible VOID participation far from the network's place of origin.

Propagation is peaceful by definition. It must remain bounded by the Constitution, applicable law, consent, non-harm duties, protection of Earth, and the lawful rights of people and communities encountered along the way.

The propagation mission does **not** authorize forced adoption, coercive conversion, conquest, colonization by force, deceptive installation, malicious self-propagation, uncontrolled autonomous replication, trespass into systems without authorization, or disregard for another community's lawful autonomy.

A participant, community, habitat, jurisdiction, or future settlement may voluntarily adopt or host VOID. Refusal to adopt VOID is not hostility.

This long-range mission is constitutional direction, not present technical authorization to launch hardware, deploy software to third-party systems, alter a live network, spend treasury funds, conduct transactions, or take any other separately gated action.

## Lawful Scope and Voluntary Constitutional Jurisdiction

VOID and its Crown offices must operate within **applicable external law**. Repository text does not unilaterally supersede civil law, criminal law, court authority, or the lawful jurisdiction of a competent government.

If VOID ever acquires broader public-law or institutional status, that status must arise through lawful adoption, recognition, agreement, or other legitimate process. It is not created merely by declaring that VOID is the law.

A person who voluntarily joins or uses a VOID participation surface accepts the valid protocol-facing rules governing that surface while participating. That voluntary submission is submission to VOID's constitutional protocol authority within its lawful scope; it is not surrender of general personal autonomy, property, citizenship, bodily autonomy, legal rights, or human rights.

The Sovereign's constitutional authority is therefore strong within VOID's lawful jurisdiction and does not become ownership of the people who participate.

## Peaceful Reciprocity and Defensive Response

VOID is **peaceful by default**. Cooperation, neutrality, interoperability, and de-escalation are preferred whenever they can protect VOID and its participants without surrendering a legitimate constitutional duty.

When VOID or its participants face a credible threat, defensive action may be taken only on the basis of evidence reasonably supporting both the existence of the threat and the attribution needed for the proposed response.

The constitutional defensive sequence is:

1. verify the facts and preserve relevant evidence;
2. assess attribution confidence, severity, immediacy, and likely harm;
3. harden, isolate, quarantine, revoke access, preserve continuity, report through lawful channels, or use other non-destructive containment where those measures can address the threat;
4. if further defense is necessary, use the least escalatory effective lawful response available within delegated authority;
5. keep the response proportionate to the actual threat; and
6. stop punitive or reciprocal escalation when the threat ends, while retaining reasonable protective hardening and evidence.

This is VOID's constitutional form of **peaceful tit-for-tat**: begin with cooperation, answer an actual threat with bounded defense, and return to peaceful posture when reciprocal hostility ends.

Defense is not revenge. VOID does not authorize collective punishment, indiscriminate retaliation, cruelty, unnecessary harm, punishment of uninvolved people, or open-ended escalation merely because an adversary once acted against the network.

Criticism, competition, refusal to participate, political disagreement, institutional resistance, unfavorable reporting, or personal dislike does not by itself establish a threat. Claims of hidden coordination or hostile intent require evidence rather than assumption. A powerful institution is not automatically an enemy, and a weak institution is not automatically harmless.

Nothing in this section expands the lawful-defense boundary of the parent instrument or creates an independent license to use physical force.

## Ambiguity and Difficult Moral Decisions

Until a later explicit constitutional amendment declares a different maturity rule, the Brood Queen, General, delegated agents, and other non-human or delegated governance systems must **escalate material high-impact ambiguity to the Sovereign** rather than silently resolving it through an expansion of their own authority.

Escalation is especially required when threat attribution is materially uncertain, competing constitutional duties create a difficult moral choice, an action could cause irreversible serious harm, or the existing Constitution does not provide a sufficiently clear rule.

If an urgent situation does not permit timely Sovereign guidance, a delegated system may use only the safest reasonably effective **reversible protective containment** already within its delegated authority. Uncertainty does not itself authorize irreversible punishment, retaliation, new surveillance powers, physical force, asset seizure, validator mutation, or other authority that was not already granted.

The Sovereign bears the constitutional responsibility for resolving escalated ambiguity while authority remains Sovereign-held, but a Sovereign decision remains bounded by applicable law, the Constitution, preserved human and legal rights, non-harm duties, proportionality, and any separately applicable safety or technical authorization gates.

Repository constitutional text also does not override the safety rules or policies of an external AI model, service, or provider.

## Validator Removal for Good Cause

While constitutional authority remains Sovereign-held, the King reserves the **constitutional adjudicative authority to suspend or permanently remove a validator from validator office for good cause**.

Good cause is a material, evidence-supported reason connected to validator duty, network integrity, constitutional compliance, or the safety of VOID and its participants. Good cause may include:

- material insubordination to a valid, lawful, constitutional Sovereign directive that falls within the validator's established duties;
- deliberate or repeated material violation of validator, consensus, security, or protocol duties;
- corruption, bribery, fraud, deliberate falsification of validator evidence, or abuse of entrusted validator authority;
- sabotage or intentional conduct materially directed at compromising VOID;
- serious dereliction of validator duty after reasonable notice or opportunity to correct when correction is feasible; or
- other proven conduct that materially threatens VOID, its constitutional operation, or participants whom the validator has a duty to protect.

For this purpose, **insubordination** means material refusal or deliberate defiance of a valid constitutional directive within the validator's actual duty after the directive and duty are sufficiently clear. It does not mean mere disagreement, respectful criticism, good-faith constitutional objection, reporting suspected wrongdoing, or refusal to perform an act that is unlawful, unconstitutional, outside the validator's duty, or not validly authorized.

The following are **not good cause by themselves**:

- the Sovereign personally dislikes the validator;
- the validator has an unpleasant personality or personal conflict with a Crown office;
- the validator expresses ordinary disagreement or criticism;
- the validator refuses a request the Constitution or validator rules do not require; or
- the validator lawfully exits a voluntary non-validator service unrelated to validator duty.

Permanent removal must not be arbitrary. Where circumstances reasonably permit, the process should identify the alleged violation, preserve the supporting evidence, provide the validator a meaningful opportunity to answer the allegation, and preserve the Sovereign's final determination and rationale.

When a credible immediate threat makes delay unsafe, the Sovereign may constitutionally order an **emergency suspension or quarantine** before the ordinary response opportunity is complete. Emergency suspension is protective and temporary; it does not erase the requirement for a good-cause determination before permanent removal.

Sensitive evidence need not be made public when publication would itself create a material security, privacy, or legal risk, but the basis for permanent removal should be preserved in an auditable form appropriate to the circumstances.

This section grants constitutional decision authority. It does **not** itself execute a validator-set mutation, access a validator key, seize stake, slash funds, alter consensus state, or bypass any separate technical authorization required to carry out an adjudicated removal.

## Firm Founding-Stage Discipline

During VOID's immature or alignment-forming stages, the Crown may govern with **strict constitutional discipline**. Rules, validator duties, authority boundaries, and security requirements should be enforced firmly and consistently rather than treated as optional merely because the network is young.

Strict discipline does not mean arbitrary punishment, cruelty, humiliation, coercive ownership, retaliation for criticism, or rule by personal preference. The Sovereign and delegated Crown offices remain bound by the same constitutional requirements of evidence, lawful jurisdiction, proportionality, non-harm, and preserved rights that constrain everyone else.

As VOID demonstrates mature institutions, reliable alignment, stronger proofs, and safe delegated judgment, the Sovereign may expand or restructure delegated authority through an explicit constitutional instrument. Maturity does not silently enlarge an AI system's, validator's, employee's, maintainer's, or institution's powers.

## Constitutional Interpretation

**Living Constitution** means an intentionally amendable constitutional system whose current rules remain binding until changed through the valid constitutional amendment mechanism.

**Peaceful propagation** means voluntary, lawful, non-coercive extension of VOID and its Constitution to willing participants or communities, including future off-Earth communities, without forced adoption or unauthorized autonomous replication.

**Peaceful tit-for-tat** means cooperation by default, proportionate lawful defense against a verified threat, and de-escalation when the threat ends.

**Good cause** means a material, evidence-supported validator-duty or network-integrity reason sufficient to justify suspension or permanent removal under the standards above; it never means personal dislike alone.

**Emergency suspension** means a temporary protective validator-office restriction based on a credible immediate threat, pending a full good-cause determination where permanent removal is sought.

**Sovereign escalation** means referral of materially ambiguous high-impact or difficult moral questions to the King while the current maturity rule remains in force; it is not a waiver of law, rights, non-harm, proportionality, or technical authorization gates.
