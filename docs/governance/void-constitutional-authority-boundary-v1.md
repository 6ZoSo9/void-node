## Core Doctrine

Authority must be legible, typed, bounded, and contestable at every layer.

Validators are not just infrastructure. Validators are protected witnesses to VOID truth.

Constitutional authority may stop the machine. It may not secretly become the machine.

Emergency authority can preserve truth, pause risk, and force review. It cannot create ordinary truth without validator-visible process.

VOID's operational independence from outside gatekeepers, investors, platforms, or operators must not be reinterpreted as constitutional independence from ZoSo while ZoSo remains Sovereign.

## Validator Constitutional Boundary

The eventual constitutional validator maximum is **144,000**.

The 144,000 ceiling is a long-horizon constitutional maximum only. It does not admit a validator, expand any current active-validator cap, change stake requirements, activate validator authority, create validator quorum, or modify current operational admission rules.

Validators remain protected witnesses to VOID truth and may be governed by a validator-specific sovereign charter. General participant governance may not silently override validator-specific constitutional protections, duties, or admission rules.

Validator participation, admission, removal, contest, quorum, and succession powers remain subject to separately defined and proven rules. The numerical ceiling alone grants none of those powers.

## Delegated AI and Participant Governance

A delegated AI governance role may administer bounded protocol-facing rules and services for voluntary non-validator VOID participation, but only under this constitution and subordinate to ZoSo's sovereign constitutional authority while ZoSo remains Sovereign.

This delegation is governance of network participation and protocol behavior, not ownership of people. It creates no real-world political authority, personal dominion, surveillance entitlement, coercive enforcement power, physical-control authority, or permission to violate law or human rights.

Joining, using, or contributing to VOID does not make a person property and does not waive that person's legal rights, bodily autonomy, or human rights.

Validators are outside this general participant-governance delegation except where their own validator charter or an explicit constitutional instrument says otherwise.

## Succession and Inheritance Are Separate

VOID governance succession and personal inheritance are separate legal and constitutional questions.

A future VOID successor does not automatically receive ZoSo's personal assets, equity, intellectual-property interests, accounts, funds, keys, or other legally personal economic interests merely by becoming VOID's constitutional successor.

ZoSo's expressed estate direction is that legally personal assets, equity, intellectual-property interests, and other personal economic interests should pass toward ZoSo's family or bloodline through valid external estate instruments and applicable law.

Family inheritance does not automatically confer VOID constitutional succession. A family member who inherits personal property does not become VOID's Sovereign unless a valid VOID succession instrument separately makes that person or body the successor.

This repository document does not itself transfer legal title, operate a wallet or signer, move assets, create a will or trust, replace beneficiary designations, override entity law, or convert network, treasury, validator, or entity property into ZoSo's personal property.

## Non-Harm Boundary

No authority created or described by this constitution authorizes killing, physical harm, coercive violence, or deprivation of human rights.

No authority created or described by this constitution authorizes intentional harm to the Earth, the sea, ecosystems, or shared environmental resources.

Protocol governance, validator governance, sovereign authority, delegated AI administration, emergency powers, and any future succession process remain bounded by these non-harm rules and by applicable law.
