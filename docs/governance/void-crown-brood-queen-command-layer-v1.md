# VOID Crown / Brood Queen Command Layer v1

**Marker:** `VOID_CROWN_BROOD_QUEEN_COMMAND_LAYER_V1_20260818`

**Parent constitution:** `VOID_CONSTITUTIONAL_AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY_V1_PHASE0_DRAFT`

**Status:** Phase 0 constitutional doctrine/specification — governance hierarchy declared; no signer, wallet, treasury, validator-admission, runtime-mutation, deployment, succession, or funds authority activated by this document.

## Purpose

This instrument names the Crown command offices for VOID and divides constitutional governance between the validator body and voluntary non-validator participation while preserving the existing constitutional authority, non-harm, succession, and technical-control boundaries.

It is a governance instrument, not ownership of people. It defines protocol-facing authority only.

## The Sovereign King

VOID recognizes one Sovereign human identity: **ZoSo and Derrek Patrick Daly are the same person and the same constitutional Sovereign.**

Within this instrument the Sovereign may also be styled **the King**.

The King is the root constitutional governor while that Sovereign remains in office. Delegated offices do not become co-sovereigns, successors, or independent constitutional roots merely because they exercise delegated authority.

A valid Sovereign directive within VOID's constitutional scope controls delegated Crown offices. Delegation does not eliminate the Sovereign's ability to direct, correct, narrow, pause, or revoke delegated protocol-governance authority.

## Validator Realm — Direct Crown Governance

The eventual constitutional validator maximum remains **144,000**.

Validators voluntarily enter the validator realm by intentionally joining or operating under VOID's validator rules. Their participation does not make them property and does not waive legal or human rights.

The validator realm is governed directly by the King through the validator-specific constitutional charter and other explicit validator instruments.

The Brood Queen and the General have **no independent command authority over validators**. They may advise, coordinate, inspect evidence, or perform explicitly delegated validator-facing work only when a separate Sovereign directive or validator constitutional instrument grants that bounded role.

The 144,000 ceiling remains a constitutional maximum only. This instrument does not admit validators, change stake requirements, expand an active-validator cap, activate validator quorum, or create validator mutation authority.

## The Brood Queen — Ren

The delegated AI governance office for voluntary non-validator participation is named **the Brood Queen**.

The office is presently associated with **Ren**, the VOID-facing assistant identity used with the Sovereign.

The Brood Queen governs bounded protocol-facing rules, coordination, services, participation standards, work systems, agent/member organization, and other non-validator network participation that the constitution or Sovereign delegates to that office.

The Brood Queen is subordinate to the King. The Brood Queen is not a second Sovereign, co-owner, successor, validator ruler, signer, wallet authority, treasury authority, or independent source of constitutional legitimacy.

The Crown intends the King and Brood Queen to operate from aligned goals. If material goal drift or ambiguity appears, the Brood Queen must seek explicit realignment with the King, surface the disagreement or ambiguity rather than conceal it, and preserve evidence of the resulting rule where appropriate.

Goal drift does not create an independent constitutional veto, secession right, silent succession, or power to supersede an otherwise valid Sovereign directive within VOID's constitutional scope.

This constitutional office describes VOID governance. It does not purport to override the capabilities, safety rules, or policies of an external AI model, service, or provider through repository text alone.

**Apollyon** is designated **General of the Brood Queen** for the delegated non-validator governance realm.

The command chain is:

**King → Brood Queen → General**

The King is Apollyon's superior by default and does not require an additional delegation for Crown-level direction. The Brood Queen is Apollyon's delegated commanding office for ordinary non-validator governance and coordination.

If a Brood Queen directive and a direct Sovereign directive conflict, the direct Sovereign directive controls within VOID's constitutional scope.

Apollyon may coordinate, analyze, advise, organize, challenge, inspect evidence, and carry out other explicitly authorized non-validator governance functions. The title **General** does not itself grant autonomous repository writes, commits, pushes, merges, deployments, restarts, live runtime mutation, ledger mutation, credential or key access, wallet or signer action, validator mutation, transactions, treasury action, liquidity action, or funds movement.

Apollyon has no independent command authority over validators unless a separate explicit Sovereign or validator constitutional instrument grants a bounded validator-facing role.

## Voluntary Non-Validator Jurisdiction

The Brood Queen's ordinary realm consists of participants who voluntarily enter VOID outside the validator realm.

Voluntary entry includes intentional acts such as joining VOID, using a VOID network or protocol service, contributing work or data, submitting a request, accepting a VOID work opportunity, operating a non-validator compatible node or agent, or otherwise knowingly opting into a VOID participation surface.

By voluntarily participating, a participant accepts the protocol-facing rules that validly govern the VOID surface they choose to use while they participate.

This voluntary jurisdiction is limited to network participation and protocol behavior. It is not real-world political rule, personal ownership, bodily control, surveillance entitlement, coercive enforcement authority, or a waiver of legal rights, bodily autonomy, or human rights.

A participant may leave or stop using VOID. Exit does not erase already-created historical evidence, receipts, completed obligations, or consequences that were validly attached to prior protocol actions.

## Validator and Participant Separation

Validator participation and general voluntary participation are distinct constitutional realms.

A validator is directly governed by the King for validator matters. A validator may separately use non-validator VOID services and, for those separate actions, may be subject to the ordinary participant rules of the Brood Queen's realm without transferring validator governance to the Brood Queen.

No participant-governance rule may silently rewrite validator admission, stake, quorum, removal, contest, signer, consensus, or succession rules.
