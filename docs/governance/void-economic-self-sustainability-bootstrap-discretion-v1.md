# VOID economic self-sustainability and WC/VOID bootstrap discretion v1

Marker: `VOID_ECONOMIC_SELF_SUSTAINABILITY_BOOTSTRAP_DISCRETION_V1`

Status: constraint-only economic doctrine. This document grants no runtime,
wallet, signer, treasury, liquidity, validator, or Chain-2050 mutation authority.

## Economic end state

VOID should be able to perform routine network operation without continuous
manual subsidy or operator babysitting.

The premine is launch capital and strategic reserve. It is not intended to be a
permanent operating fuel source.

As real network usage grows, ordinary protocol revenue, fees, market activity,
and other separately approved economic mechanisms should increasingly carry
routine operating costs and incentives. Routine validator reward accounting,
fee accounting/distribution, maintenance accounting, ordinary settlement, health
monitoring/bounded recovery, and validator consensus under standing rules should
be automated after their own implementation, proof, and activation gates.

This is an automation objective, not an authority shortcut.

## WC/VOID bootstrap liquidity

The existing initial WC/VOID opening inventory policy remains unchanged.

Additional VOID does **not** have to be added merely because WC is issued,
because the pool reaches a published reserve level, or because a price moves.

Supplemental WC/VOID liquidity during bootstrap remains Sovereign-discretionary.

There is no automatic treasury top-up trigger, public reserve threshold,
depletion threshold, refill cadence, price-support formula, peg, or guaranteed
treasury replenishment rule.

WC holders and market participants have no entitlement to a treasury top-up.

The Sovereign may decide that additional VOID liquidity is useful during the
bootstrap phase, may decide that no addition is needed, or may stop supplemental
support. The decision may consider current market conditions without publishing
a deterministic strategy that participants can target.

Every actual treasury/liquidity movement still requires its own applicable
authority, custody, transaction, evidence, and accounting gates. This doctrine
does not itself move funds.

Supplemental discretionary liquidity is a bootstrap mechanism, not the intended
steady-state operating model.

## Organic WC demand and two-way exchange

WC remains useful-work accounting value rather than a fixed claim on VOID.

There is no fixed WC-to-VOID conversion or redemption ratio.

Where a WC/VOID market is separately enabled, exchange may occur in either
direction at a market-determined price. Market participants acquiring WC with
VOID may naturally return VOID inventory to the market, so intentional treasury
top-ups may be unnecessary.

WC is also intended to become usable for real goods and services. Accordingly,
future demand may include direct exchange of WC for goods and services rather
than routing every use through VOID.

Neither use case creates a guaranteed treasury redemption obligation.

## Automation boundary

The desired steady state is that routine network economics and operations should
become protocol-automated within already-approved rules.

Automation should reduce routine manual intervention; it must not erase the
constitutional truth boundary.

Automation does not absorb Sovereign-reserved discretion. In particular, this
doctrine does not automate:

- constitutional amendment;
- authority expansion;
- supplemental WC/VOID treasury liquidity;
- emergency treasury spending;
- exceptional key recovery or authority-bearing rotation; or
- any other act separately reserved to Sovereign discretion.

Ordinary automation operates under the last valid rules. Changing those rules is
a different authority class.

## Success condition

The economic design succeeds when ordinary network activity can sustain routine
operation without recurring premine withdrawals, while the premine can remain
primarily strategic/bootstrap reserve and the WC/VOID market can function from
real two-way demand.

This is a direction of architecture and policy. It does not claim that current
Mainnet-0 has already reached that steady state.

## Authority boundary

This doctrine does not:

- activate a market;
- fund a pool;
- transfer treasury assets;
- set a WC/VOID price;
- promise a top-up;
- create a fixed redemption;
- issue WC;
- activate validators;
- sign or broadcast a transaction;
- mutate Chain-2050; or
- amend constitutional authority.
